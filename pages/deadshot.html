Overview

Deadshot is a first-person sniper game where you play an elite marksman defending critical city infrastructure from escalating drone attacks. Across five missions you must hold the line as swarms grow larger and more aggressive, scanning the skies, compensating for wind and gravity, and picking your shots through rain, fog, and shifting conditions. It's tense, focused, and deeply satisfying when everything clicks.

The game follows a clean progression arc: new locations, tougher enemies, and unlockable rifles that can be upgraded as you advance. Each weapon opens up a new way to approach the threat, rewarding players who push through and master the game's escalating challenge.

Key Features

01

Realistic Ballistics

Every shot is affected by wind and gravity — line up your trajectory, account for the conditions, and make it count.

02

Dynamic Weather

Rain, wind, and fog shift the battlefield in real time, obscuring targets and forcing you to adapt your approach on the fly.

Unlock three distinct sniper rifles as you progress and upgrade them to match your playstyle and the threats ahead.

Escalating Threat

Drone swarms grow smarter and more numerous with each level — what works early won't save you later.

Each mission takes place at a different piece of city infrastructure, bringing a new layout, sightlines, and tactical challenge.
