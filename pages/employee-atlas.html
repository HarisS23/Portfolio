Overview

Employee Atlas is a full-stack web application for managing everything that happens inside an organisation from hiring and promotions to leave requests and resignations. It gives both employees and managers a single platform to handle the workflows that usually get lost in emails and spreadsheets.

The core idea was to replace informal, scattered HR processes with a structured system that actually tracks things end to end. Every request whether it's a leave, an elevation, or a resignation goes through a proper approval flow, with live notifications keeping both sides informed at every step.

Built with ASP.NET Core and Angular, the project covers real-time features via SignalR, role-based access for employees and admins, and an internal job board where open positions can be listed and applied to directly through the platform.

Key Features

01

Employee Management

Admins can hire, fire, and promote employees directly through the platform. Role and department changes are reflected immediately across the system.

02

Work Hour Tracking

Employees log their daily working hours through the app. Managers get a clear overview of team attendance and time spent without chasing spreadsheets.

03

Live Notifications

Real-time notifications keep employees and managers in sync. Request approvals, status updates, and announcements are pushed instantly without a page refresh.

04

Job Board & Applications

Open positions are listed on an internal job board. Employees and external candidates can browse and apply directly through the platform.

05

Leave Requests

Employees submit leave requests through a structured flow. Managers review and approve or reject them, with the outcome notified in real time.

06

Resignation & Elevation

Employees can formally submit resignation or elevation requests through the app. Each request enters an approval workflow tracked end to end.
